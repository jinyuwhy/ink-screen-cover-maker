## Open Library

Book search and cover lookup use the public, low-volume APIs provided by [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api). Search requests are initiated by the user and cached for the duration of the application session. A result is labelled “Open Library”, and double-clicking it opens the corresponding Open Library page when available.

Open Library records and cover images may have their own rights and attribution requirements. The application does not bundle or redistribute them.
